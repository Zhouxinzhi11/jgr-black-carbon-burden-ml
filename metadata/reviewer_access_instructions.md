# Reviewer access instructions

1. The fused-data record is separate from the public software record and remains an unpublished restricted Zenodo draft (reserved DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23139189`).
2. Its metadata may be visible after publication, but files remain controlled under the custom restricted-rights statement; MIT and CC BY 4.0 do not apply.
3. A reviewer preview secret link has been generated and stored outside Git, both staging trees, and both ZIP archives. Transmit it only through the journal's confidential editor/reviewer channel.
4. Never place the secret link in the manuscript, README, GitHub, Zenodo public metadata, manifests, or reports. Revoke or rotate a disclosed link.
5. After publication, researchers may submit a reasoned scientific-use application. Access is decided by the corresponding author, in consultation with the data creators, conditional on an editor-approved restricted-access exception and the applicable data-rights decision; a no-redistribution agreement may be required.
6. The public software DOI (`10.5281/zenodo.23139187`) and restricted-data DOI (`10.5281/zenodo.23139189`) identify distinct records. Both records are currently unpublished drafts.

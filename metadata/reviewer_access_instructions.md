# Reviewer access instructions

1. The main fused-data record (`10.5281/zenodo.23139189`) and transport recovery supplement (`10.5281/zenodo.23463888`) are published separately from the public software record. Both data records have restricted files.
2. Their metadata are public, but files remain controlled under the custom restricted-rights statement; MIT and CC BY 4.0 do not apply. Neither has an automatic public-release embargo.
3. Read-only reviewer preview links are managed outside Git and the published archives. Access to both records is needed for the complete 96-part logical delivery. Transmit links only through the journal's confidential editor/reviewer channel.
4. Never place the secret link in the manuscript, README, GitHub, Zenodo public metadata, manifests, or reports. Revoke or rotate a disclosed link.
5. After publication, researchers may submit a reasoned scientific-use application. Access is decided by the corresponding author, in consultation with the data creators, conditional on an editor-approved restricted-access exception and the applicable data-rights decision; a no-redistribution agreement may be required.
6. The software DOI (`10.5281/zenodo.23139187`) and two data DOIs identify distinct published records. Publication of the data metadata does not make their files public or establish approval of the requested editorial exception.

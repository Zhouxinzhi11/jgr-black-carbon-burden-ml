# Data and Software Availability

The author-written analysis code, formal-run and provenance manifests, environment files, public aggregate results, and non-sensitive table/figure source-data CSV files are prepared in the private GitHub repository <https://github.com/Zhouxinzhi11/jgr-black-carbon-burden-ml> and an unpublished Zenodo software draft with reserved DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23139187>. The code is licensed under the MIT License; public aggregate results and public table/figure source-data CSV files are licensed under CC BY 4.0. These records will become publicly accessible only after final release authorization.

GF-5(02) POSP Level 1 is a public upstream data product obtained through CRESDA (<https://data.cresda.cn/>); account registration may be required. In this study, POSP I380 is used only to define record availability. The public POSP Level 2 product and DOI `10.57760/sciencedb.14748` were not used as the source of I380 for this analysis.

The restricted objects are the POSP-centered analysis-ready fused derivative; row-level or grid-cell-level targets and predictors; exact spatial-temporal support; arrays capable of reconstructing those contents; and the Figure 3 and Figure S3 pointwise/cellwise spatial arrays. POSP Level 1 records themselves remain public upstream records. The full fused derivative is prepared for editors and reviewers through a separate restricted-access Zenodo draft with reserved DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23139189>. After publication, researchers may submit a reasoned scientific-use application. Access will be decided by the corresponding author, in consultation with the data creators, conditional on an editor-approved restricted-access exception and the applicable data-rights decision; recipients may be required to agree not to redistribute the fused data. This controlled-access route is more specific than an unqualified "available upon request" statement.

Public aggregate CSV files reproduce Main Tables 1–4 and SI Tables S1–S12. Public aggregate figure source data support Main Figures 1–5 and SI Figures S1–S3. For Main Figure 3 and SI Figure S3, pointwise/cellwise source arrays are available only through the restricted-access record, subject to the approved access conditions.

Full training, large preprocessing, and restricted-data scientific workflows were not rerun during release preparation. The GitHub repository remains private and both Zenodo records remain unpublished drafts at this stage.
